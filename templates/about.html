About me



A small summary about Aveeraj Singh Chahal
Hello, my name is Aveeraj, but a lot of people call me Avee as it's easier to pronounce. This page is dedicated to my hobbies as well as the things I find interesting.
After graduating from the University of Huddersfield with a First Class in Cyber Security, I found myself wanting to develop my skills through different projects that I find interesting. Some of the more complex projects I've programmed include:

- A camera detection program that tracks the user's hand and lets them draw on the screen using their fingers.

- This website, which I built myself from scratch with very little prior knowledge of HTML and CSS.

You can get more details about these projects, and others I've made, on my Projects and blog pages.

One thing I found particularly interesting was building my own PC to use for creating these projects. Researching the different components was a great way to learn about hardware, and just how much of an impact different parts can have on a system's performance.

Outside of technology, some of my other hobbies include rugby union and going to the gym. These physically intense hobbies are a great way for me to switch off and clear my head, and they help me manage any mental pressure I might be dealing with at the time. Rugby has also been a great way to find a community of friendly people and make connections outside of work.
